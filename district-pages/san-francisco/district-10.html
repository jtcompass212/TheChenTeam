District 10 Homes & Real Estate

District 10 is the southeast quarter — Bayview, Hunters Point, the Excelsior, Portola and Visitacion Valley. It is the most attainable single-family district in San Francisco and, by sale count, one of the busiest: the stock is overwhelmingly detached houses on standard lots, much of it built between the 1920s and the 1950s, on some of the sunniest ground in the city. Below you’ll find current homes for sale across District 10, updated throughout the day.
