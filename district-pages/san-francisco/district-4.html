District 4 Homes & Real Estate

District 4 is the arc of planned residence parks on the western slope of Twin Peaks — St Francis Wood, Forest Hill, Balboa Terrace, Westwood Park and their neighbours. Laid out in the 1910s and 1920s with curving streets, mature street trees and design covenants, these are the closest thing San Francisco has to garden suburbs, and the stock is correspondingly large.
