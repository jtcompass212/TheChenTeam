District 1 Homes & Real Estate

District 1 is San Francisco's northwest quarter — the long grid of the Richmond running from Arguello out to Ocean Beach, bounded by the Presidio above and Golden Gate Park below. It is a district of wide, flat avenues and solid pre-war stock: Edwardian flats and marina-style homes on 25-foot lots, giving way to the large detached houses and ocean frontage of Sea Cliff and Lake Street.
