District 3 Homes & Real Estate

District 3 is the city's southwest corner — Lakeside, Ingleside, Oceanview and the blocks around Lake Merced and San Francisco State. It is among the most attainable single-family markets in San Francisco, with a mix of 1920s bungalows, post-war tract housing and larger lots than the districts to the north.
