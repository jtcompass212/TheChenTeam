District 6 Homes & Real Estate

District 6 runs across the middle of the city's northern half — Hayes Valley, Alamo Square, the North Panhandle and the Western Addition. It is one of the densest districts in San Francisco and overwhelmingly a flats-and-condominium market; detached single-family houses are the exception here rather than the rule, which is why the figures below rest on a small number of sales.
