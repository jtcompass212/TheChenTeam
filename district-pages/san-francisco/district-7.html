District 7 Homes & Real Estate

District 7 is the city's most expensive district and has been for a long time — Pacific Heights, Presidio Heights, Cow Hollow and the Marina, running along the northern waterfront from the Presidio to Van Ness. The stock ranges from Marina-style flats on the flat blocks near the bay to the very large detached houses on the Pacific Heights crest, where the Golden Gate views are.
