District 8 Homes & Real Estate

District 8 covers the city's dense northeast corner — Russian Hill, Nob Hill, Telegraph Hill, North Beach and the Financial District. It is the most vertical district in San Francisco and almost entirely a condominium and co-operative market; the handful of detached single-family sales each quarter are unusual properties, so the figures below describe a very small slice of what actually trades here. Below you’ll find current homes for sale across District 8, updated throughout the day.
