District 2 Homes & Real Estate

District 2 is the Sunset and Parkside — the largest contiguous residential district in San Francisco and, by volume, its busiest single-family market. The stock is remarkably consistent: rows of 1930s and 1940s tunnel-entrance houses on a regular grid, most with garages below and level entries, running from Golden Gate Park south to Sloat and west to the ocean.
