# Redwood City neighborhood pages — sources

Prose on the 13 Redwood City pages is written for this site. Sources below were
used for facts only. No wording was carried across.

## Neighborhoods

- [Burlingame Properties — Redwood City](https://burlingameproperties.com/communities/redwood-city)
  — Central Park east of El Camino covering Palm Park and Woodside Plaza;
  Clifford Heights as an unincorporated pocket under 1,000 residents, quieter
  than Emerald Lake Hills; Cordilleras Heights in Emerald Hills with large
  hillside homes on narrow winding streets; Farm Hill Estates as the city's
  westernmost district on large wooded lots; Horgan Ranch closest to Atherton;
  Centennial as one of the "ABC streets" with Stambaugh-Heller and Lenolt.
- [DeLeon Realty — Mount Carmel](https://deleonrealty.com/neighborhoods/redwood-city/mount-carmel/)
  and [Walking Redwood City — Eagle Hill](https://walkingredwoodcity.com/2018/12/14/neighborhoods-eagle-hill/)
  — Mt. Carmel's early-1900s to mid-century housing on large level lots; Eagle
  Hill as so similar to Mt. Carmel that the boundary is hard to place, almost
  entirely residential apart from a school and a few churches.
- Mt. Carmel and Edgewood Park as the pre-war core, with 1920s–30s Craftsman,
  Tudor and Spanish Revival under heritage street trees.
- Horgan Ranch's Redwood Oaks pocket ranging from ~1,000 sq ft houses to
  custom builds of 3,000 sq ft and up.
- Redwood Shores in the northeastern corner, home to the Oracle campus,
  Electronic Arts and DreamWorks Animation.

## Downtown and Bair Island

- [Visit California — Redwood City](https://www.visitcalifornia.com/places-to-visit/redwood-city/)
  — the 1929 Fox Theatre, a former movie palace on the National Register,
  anchoring the revived Courthouse Square; the Bair Island Ecological Reserve
  and Don Edwards San Francisco Bay National Wildlife Refuge, roughly 3,000
  acres of marsh with an observation deck on Middle Bair Island.

## Schools

- [Sequoia Union High School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequoia_Union_High_School_District)
  and [Sequoia High School](https://www.sequoiahs.org/ABOUT/Campus-History/index.html)
  — Sequoia High founded 1895, downtown; Woodside High founded 1958.
  Elementary and middle grades are run by the Redwood City School District,
  except Redwood Shores which is Belmont-Redwood Shores.

**Note:** school cards name schools serving the city. They do not assert
attendance-boundary assignment, which was not verified per neighborhood.

## Two pages publish no figures

- **Bair Island** — a wildlife refuge, not a neighborhood. Compass does record
  three "sales" against the name in 2026-Q2, but a tidal marsh reserve has no
  housing; those are almost certainly adjacent Redwood Shores condominiums
  mis-attributed. Publishing a $1.27M median under this heading would be wrong.
- **Downtown Redwood City** — the name exists in Compass but almost nothing is
  attributed to it: zero sales in both 2024 and 2025, and never more than three
  in a year. Sales in the downtown blocks are recorded under adjoining
  neighborhoods.

## Property-type cut

Two neighborhoods publish all-property-type figures because detached housing is
marginal: **Central Park** (0% of 2025 sales were detached) and **Centennial**.
See `docs/deriving-market-figures.md` for the rule.

Central Park is worth noting — Compass records no detached sales there at all,
across every year on record, despite 15–24 attached sales a year. The page
reports the condominium market because that is the market.
