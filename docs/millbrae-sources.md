# Millbrae neighborhood pages — sources

Prose on the 13 Millbrae pages is written for this site. These sources were
used for the underlying facts only — build eras, boundaries, school and park
names, addresses, and the two historical episodes (the Macco Pit haul to the
SFO runway, and the 1968 freeway proposal through Millwood). No wording was
carried across.

## Neighborhood history and boundaries

- [LookyLoo — Millbrae neighborhoods](https://lookyloomove.com/locations/millbrae/neighborhoods)
  — Bayside Manor's 1945 founding, six-block extent, house sizes; Capuchino's
  farm origins and commercial strip; Green Hills' mid-1970s build-out and
  creeks; Mills Estate's mid-1950s hillside development.
- [Burlingame Properties — Millbrae](https://burlingameproperties.com/communities/millbrae)
  — Glenview Highlands as the newest housing stock; Highlands' pre-war
  Spanish/Mission Revival, cottages and Tudors; Lomita Hills at the San Bruno
  line on Santa Lucia Avenue; Millbrae Meadows' position west of I-280.
- [Burlingame Properties — Telescope Hills & Silva Ranch](https://burlingameproperties.com/neighborhoods/telescope-hills-and-silva-ranch)
  — the Macco Pit, Richmond Drive haul route, Silva Tract, and the late-1950s
  to 1970s build-out.
- [Burlingame Properties — Millwood](https://burlingameproperties.com/neighborhoods/millwood)
  — flower-growing origins, early-1950s subdivision after the 1948
  incorporation, and the 1968 freeway opposition.

## Schools

- [Millbrae Elementary School District — Schools](https://www.millbraeschooldistrict.org/Schools)
  — the district is TK–8 and operates five schools: Green Hills, Lomita Park,
  Meadows and Spring Valley elementaries, plus Taylor Middle School.
- [Mills High School — SMUHSD](https://mhs.smuhsd.org/students/student-and-parent-handbook/school-and-district-contact-information)
  — 400 Murchison Drive, San Mateo Union High School District.

**Note:** school cards name schools in the districts serving Millbrae. They do
not assert attendance-boundary assignment, which was not verified per
neighborhood and should be confirmed before relying on it.

## Parks and civic

- [City of Millbrae — Parks](https://www.ci.millbrae.ca.us/316/Parks)
  — Central Park, Green Hills Park, Marina Vista Park, Bayside Manor Park,
  Meadows Park; the Spur Trail as the city's Class I path.
- [San Mateo County Libraries — Millbrae](https://smcl.org/locations/1m/)
  — 1 Library Avenue.

## Downtown dining

## Open questions

Two items on these pages are **not** fully verified and are flagged here
rather than buried:

1. **"Highlands" in Compass.** Millbrae Highlands is the local name, and
   Compass lists "Highlands" separately from "San Mateo Highlands", so the
   match is probable — but the API gives no way to confirm which city a
   neighborhood belongs to. Filtering by city and neighborhood together
   returns citywide totals rather than the intersection. The figures on
   `highlands.html` rest on that unconfirmed match.

2. **Mills Estates spans two cities.** Compass has one "Mills Estates"; both
   the Millbrae and Burlingame maps claim it, and the estate it is named for
   straddled the line. `burlingame/mills-estates.html` and
   `millbrae/mills-estates.html` therefore publish identical figures drawn
   from the same Compass neighborhood. That is arguably correct — it is one
   real place — but it is a deliberate duplication, not an error to fix.
