# Foster City neighborhood pages — sources

Prose on the 10 Foster City pages is written for this site. These sources were
used for facts only — build eras, developers, boundaries, school and park
names. No wording was carried across.

## City history

- [San Francisco Peninsula — Foster City history](https://thesanfranciscopeninsula.com/about-us/san-mateo-county-history/foster-city)
  — the land was marshland known as Brewer Island; T. Jack Foster's 1958 plan
  for a master-planned waterfront community; 2.5 million yards of fill to
  create the 218-acre lagoon; $4.7M of Foster's own money committed by 1962.

## Neighborhoods

- [Burlingame Properties — Foster City](https://burlingameproperties.com/communities/foster-city)
  and its per-neighborhood pages for
  [Treasure Isle](https://burlingameproperties.com/neighborhoods/treasure-isle),
  [Isle Cove](https://burlingameproperties.com/neighborhoods/isle-cove) and
  [Sea Colony](https://burlingameproperties.com/neighborhoods/sea-colony)
  — Treasure Isle built 1963–65 by Kay Builders, Duc and Elliot, and Joseph
  Eichler, with streets named for ships; Bay Vista as the second planned
  neighborhood from 1965; Carmel Village built alongside it with a mix of
  housing types; Marina Point as neighborhood #4, south of Foster City
  Boulevard between Beach Park and Shell; Sea Colony as the largest and most
  water-oriented, containing Sea Cloud Park; Isle Cove as the last of the nine
  planned neighborhoods, condo-heavy, with streets named for stars and
  constellations; Harbor Side's private docks and ~2,500 sq ft two-story homes;
  The Islands' 174-unit complex and its 1976 Award of Excellence from
  Architectural Record.

## Schools

- [San Mateo-Foster City School District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Mateo-Foster_City_School_District)
  and [City of Foster City — schools](https://www.fostercity.org/citycouncil/page/let%E2%80%99s-talk-about-our-schools)
  — Brewer Island Elementary at 1151 Polynesia Drive; Bowditch Middle School.
  Foster City has no high school of its own: students attend San Mateo,
  Hillsdale or Aragon in the San Mateo Union High School District depending on
  address.

**Note:** school cards name schools serving Foster City. They do not assert
attendance-boundary assignment, which was not verified per neighborhood.

## Vintage Park is not residential

`vintage-park` publishes no market figures. The area around Vintage Park Drive
and Chess Drive is Foster City's office district — the
[Gilead Sciences campus](https://www.commercialcafe.com/commercial-property/us/ca/foster-city/gilead-sciences-campus-353-393-vintage-park-drive/)
occupies much of it — and Compass records no residential sales there in any
period. Same treatment as Green Hills Country Club in Millbrae.

## Property-type cut

Three neighborhoods publish all-property-type figures because detached housing
is marginal in them: Isle Cove (6% of 2025 sales), The Islands (7%) and Marina
Point (9%). The reasoning and the full share table are in
`docs/deriving-market-figures.md`.

Marina Point is worth flagging: other sources describe it as family cul-de-sac
housing, and it does have detached houses, but three of its thirty-three 2025
sales were detached. Its page says so rather than publishing a $2.46M
single-family median that would describe a tenth of the market.
