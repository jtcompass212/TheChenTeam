About the Community

From orchards to the epicenter of Silicon Valley

Sunnyvale incorporated in 1912, back when the land was still fruit orchards. Today it's home to major offices for Google, LinkedIn, and Apple, alongside quiet family neighborhoods, a strong Eichler home inventory, and a historic downtown along Murphy Avenue built around a Saturday farmers market that draws crowds from across the valley.

History

From fruit orchards to Fortune 500

Pioneer Martin Murphy Jr. is credited with planting the area's first orchards, and Sunnyvale incorporated in 1912 as an agricultural town in what was then called the Valley of Heart's Delight. The Southern Pacific Railroad arrived in 1904, and by the 1930s a naval airfield — later Moffett Field — brought the aerospace industry to town.

World War II shifted the local economy from farming toward manufacturing and defense, setting the stage for Sunnyvale's postwar transformation into a founding center of Silicon Valley's technology industry.
