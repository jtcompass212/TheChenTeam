About the Community

The Peninsula's working capital, reinvented

Redwood City's downtown has transformed over the past decade into a genuine walkable village around Courthouse Square, while the rest of the city spans everything from Oracle's waterfront campus in Redwood Shores to quiet, wooded streets in Emerald Hills. As the county seat, it also carries the civic weight — and the housing variety — that comes with being San Mateo County's oldest incorporated city.

History

From Mezesville to the county seat

Founded in 1854 as Mezesville by land claimant Simon Mezes, the settlement grew into a lumber-shipping port — its natural deep-water channel made it the ideal outlet for the redwood that rebuilt San Francisco after the Gold Rush, which is how it earned its present name.

When San Mateo County formed in 1856, Redwood City became its seat, and it incorporated in 1867 — the first city in the county to do so. The Port of Redwood City remains the only active deep-water port on the South Bay.
