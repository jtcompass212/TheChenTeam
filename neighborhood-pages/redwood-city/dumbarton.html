Dumbarton Homes & Real Estate

Dumbarton is one of the larger MLS districts in Redwood City and takes in several different pockets, so its character shifts from block to block more than most neighborhoods on this map. It covers the ground between the downtown core and the bay side of the city, which means the housing ranges from older cottages to newer infill within a short walk.
