Parrot Drive Area Homes & Real Estate

The Parrott Drive area takes its name from the estate family whose land it was, and it runs through some of the more secluded ground in the town. Even by Hillsborough standards the houses here sit far back from the road behind gates and tree rows, which is precisely what the town’s founding rules were written to produce.
