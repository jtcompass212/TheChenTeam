Hillsborough · Homeplace

Homeplace Homes & Real Estate

Homeplace is one of the fourteen designated neighborhoods the town recognizes, on ground subdivided from the estates in the middle of the last century. It has what the whole of Hillsborough has — half-acre lots, no sidewalks, no shops — and, like its neighbors, a market measured in a handful of sales a year rather than a month.
