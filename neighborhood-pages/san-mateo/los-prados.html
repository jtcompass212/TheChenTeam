Los Prados Homes & Real Estate

The last of San Mateo's eastern neighborhoods to be subdivided, Los Prados grew up around its namesake park near Marina Lagoon starting in the early 1960s — it's rare to find a home here built before then. The result is a compact, walkable mix of single-family houses, townhomes, and HOA-maintained condominium courtyards, which keeps it one of the more attainable ways to buy near the water in San Mateo.
