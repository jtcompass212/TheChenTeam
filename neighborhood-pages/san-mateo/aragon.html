Aragon Homes & Real Estate

Annexed by San Mateo in 1929, Aragon is a storied pocket of tree-lined boulevards, nostalgic lamp posts, and large pre-war Spanish Revival, Tudor, and English-style homes built mostly between 1930 and 1940. It's one of the rare San Mateo neighborhoods where a family can walk K–12: Aragon High School sits right at 900 Alameda de las Pulgas, inside the neighborhood's own boundaries.
