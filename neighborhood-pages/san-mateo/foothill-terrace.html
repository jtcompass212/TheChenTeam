Foothill Terrace Homes & Real Estate

Tucked into the rolling hills of northwest San Mateo alongside Baywood Knolls, Foothill Terrace pairs single-story 1950s ranch homes on its lower streets with larger two-story view homes higher up — some with Bay vistas — built through the 1960s and ’70s. It's long been the value-minded way into the same Baywood Elementary and Aragon High School attendance areas that anchor pricier neighborhoods nearby.
