San Mateo · Eastern Addition

Eastern Addition Homes & Real Estate

Just north across Peninsula Avenue from Bowie Estate, Eastern Addition is San Mateo's other classic entry point for first-time buyers — a patchwork of housing styles spanning nearly a century, from 1920s bungalows to 1970s infill, all within an easy walk of the B Street dining corridor and the San Mateo Caltrain station. It shares Bowie Estate's relaxed, unpretentious character and its proximity to Coyote Point, without asking buyers to compromise on being genuinely close to downtown.
