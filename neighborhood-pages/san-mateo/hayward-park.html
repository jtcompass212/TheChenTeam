Hayward Park Homes & Real Estate

Named for the 1880s estate it was built on, Hayward Park is a tree-lined pocket of Craftsman, Tudor, and Mediterranean-style homes dating to the early 1900s through the 1920s, sited just steps from downtown San Mateo. It's genuinely walkable to the Hayward Park Caltrain station and to San Mateo Central Park's Japanese Garden — a rare double of transit convenience and green space this close to the city center.
