Harbor Town Homes & Real Estate

Stretching nearly a full square mile from Seal Slough to San Francisco Bay along the Foster City border, Harbor Town is one of San Mateo's few true waterfront neighborhoods. Harbortown proper is known for 1979-built Craftsman-sided townhomes wrapped around a private lagoon, while neighboring Mariner's Isle mixes vintage 1960s-70s homes on Fathom Drive with condos and newer townhouses along Port and Shoal Drives.
