San Mateo Woods/Bayridge Homes & Real Estate

Tucked into San Mateo's far southwest corner, just west of the College of San Mateo at the end of Hillsdale Boulevard, San Mateo Woods/Bayridge is built around its namesake 1974–75 townhome development — two-story condos wrapped in cedar and mature oaks along Bayridge Way. Behind the complex, a quiet handful of 1970s–80s single-family homes on streets like Randall Road round out one of San Mateo's most low-key, HOA-managed corners.
