Baywood Knolls Homes & Real Estate

Tucked into the rolling hills of northwest San Mateo, Baywood Knolls is a small, close-knit pocket of tree-lined streets and well-kept post-war homes bordering Foothill Terrace. It's a quieter, more attainable way into the same Baywood-Aragon school pipeline that anchors San Mateo's most prestigious west-side addresses, without giving up the wooded, semi-rural feel that comes from sitting right at the base of the Crystal Springs hills.
