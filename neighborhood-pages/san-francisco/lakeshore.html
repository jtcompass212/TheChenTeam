Lakeside sits on curving streets near Lake Merced, with established homes on lots larger than most of the city's west side.

Stonestown surrounds the galleria and San Francisco State, and is largely apartments and newer development rather than detached houses.

Pinelake Park

Pinelake Park is a small pocket beside Stern Grove at the district's southern edge, backing onto the lake and the eucalyptus canyon.
