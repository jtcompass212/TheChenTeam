Outer Mission Homes & Real Estate

The Outer Mission runs between the Excelsior and Balboa Park along Mission Street, with modest homes on a flat grid. It is one of the more affordable single-family markets in the city. Below you’ll find current homes for sale in Outer Mission, updated throughout the day.
