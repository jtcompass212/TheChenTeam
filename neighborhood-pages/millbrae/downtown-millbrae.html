Downtown Millbrae Homes & Real Estate

Broadway is the reason to be here. Millbrae's downtown is a compact, low-rise stretch that has become one of the better-known places on the Peninsula to eat Chinese food — Lai Lai has held its spot at 334 Broadway since 1983, and Wonderful, at number 270, carries a Michelin listing. Housing runs mainly to condominiums and townhomes, and the BART and Caltrain platforms are close enough that a car is genuinely optional.
