Beverly Terrace Homes & Real Estate

The largest of the San Carlos hill neighborhoods, Beverly Terrace runs up the canyons above town and holds the widest range of housing in the city — early cabins, mid-century moderns, and later infill on steep, winding lots. Its oddest survivor is the octagonal house at 125 Dale Avenue, built in 1872 as a party house by Nathaniel Brittan, whose name is still on half the local landmarks.
