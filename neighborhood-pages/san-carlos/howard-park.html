San Carlos · Howard Park

Howard Park Homes & Real Estate

One of the original San Carlos neighborhoods, Howard Park dates to the first push by the San Carlos Park Syndicate and Frederick Drake, the developer local histories call the father of the city. The town’s first schoolhouse opened here in 1918, and the neighborhood still sits close enough to Laurel Street that most errands are a walk rather than a drive.
