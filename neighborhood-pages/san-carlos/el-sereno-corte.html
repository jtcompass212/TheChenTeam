El Sereno Corte Homes & Real Estate

During the 1930s downturn this was the cheap end of San Carlos, and the way developers responded still shapes it: lots were subdivided down, many to no more than 5,000 square feet, producing a denser grid of smaller houses than the hill neighborhoods above. That density is now part of the appeal — it is one of the few parts of the city where the housing stock turns over often enough to have a real entry point.
