Belmont · McDougal

McDougal Homes & Real Estate

A quiet, semi-rural neighborhood on Belmont's eastern hills, McDougal ranges from clustered apartment buildings near the flats to isolated hilltop estates tucked among wooded, dirt-trail hillsides. Low traffic and abundant open green space make it one of the more dog-friendly, nature-oriented pockets of the city, even with a housing stock that spans large custom estates to more modest homes.
