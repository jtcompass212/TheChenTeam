San Bruno · Crestmoor

Crestmoor Homes & Real Estate

Crestmoor went up on former ranch land west of I-280 from the early 1950s, and it is the clearest expression of what San Bruno built when it moved uphill: California mid-century raised ranches and split-levels, put up between 1950 and 1970 on curving streets that follow the contours rather than fight them. It is the busiest hillside market in the city. Below you’ll find current homes for sale in Crestmoor, updated throughout the day.
