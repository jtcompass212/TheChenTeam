Burlingame · Easton Addition

Easton Addition Homes & Real Estate

One of Burlingame's most prestigious pockets, Easton Addition is lined with vintage estates from the early 1900s on generous 6,000-to-10,000-plus-square-foot lots. It's also home to the Easton Branch Library, a 1927 former women's-club building that's operated as a neighborhood library annex since 1943.
