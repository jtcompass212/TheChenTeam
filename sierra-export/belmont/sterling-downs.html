Sterling Downs Homes & Real Estate

Built after 1951 on the site of the old Belmont Airport — and, during World War II, an Army camp guarding the coast — Sterling Downs is a flat, highly walkable neighborhood of postwar ranch cottages and apartments east of El Camino Real and north of Ralston Avenue. Continuous sidewalks on nearly every street make it Belmont's most pedestrian-friendly neighborhood, with Alexander Park and St. Mark Catholic Church anchoring the community.
