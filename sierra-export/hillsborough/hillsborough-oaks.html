Hillsborough Oaks Homes & Real Estate

Named for the oaks that predate every house on it, Hillsborough Oaks is a wooded pocket on the town’s western side. The planting here is old enough that the neighborhood reads as woodland with houses in it rather than the other way round, which is close to what the town has always been aiming for.
