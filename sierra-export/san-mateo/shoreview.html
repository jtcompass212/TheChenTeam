Shoreview Homes & Real Estate

Originally developed for San Mateo's working-class community, Shoreview remains one of the city's more affordable single-family neighborhoods despite its direct bay access — a rare combination on the Peninsula. Residents swim, fish, and windsurf right off the neighborhood's edge, and the Bayside Performing Arts Center hosts a steady calendar of local art, dance, and music open to the public. It's a genuine entry point for young professionals and first-time buyers who want real waterfront without the waterfront price tag.
