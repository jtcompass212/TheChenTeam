San Mateo Terrace/Beresford Homes & Real Estate

Sitting north of Hillsdale Boulevard and west of Alameda de las Pulgas, San Mateo Terrace/Beresford was carved out of the 150-acre former Beresford Estate starting in 1947, with two-, three- and four-bedroom homes originally priced between $12,000 and $20,000. The neighborhood is bookended by Hillsdale High School on one side and the Peninsula (formerly Beresford) Country Club on the other, and remains one of the more popular west-side pockets for families drawn to its quiet, single-family streets.
