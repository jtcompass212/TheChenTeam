Beresford Manor Homes & Real Estate

Tucked between Highway 92 and 27th Avenue in south-central San Mateo, Beresford Manor grew organically from the 1930s through the 1950s as one of the city's earliest annexed neighborhoods — a patchwork of Tudor cottages, Mediterranean bungalows, and traditional family homes on tidy 5,000-square-foot lots. Its true anchor is Beresford Park, whose lighted tennis and bocce courts, playgrounds, and community garden give the neighborhood a small-town feel just steps from Hillsdale Shopping Center.
