Hillsdale "The Lanes" Homes & Real Estate

Tucked north of Hillsdale Boulevard and east of El Camino Real, Hillsdale "The Lanes" is a small, hidden residential pocket that emerged during San Mateo's 1940s–50s Hillsdale build-out, developed in large part by David Bohannon, who also built the Hillsdale Shopping Center a short walk away. Ranch-style homes with attached garages line quiet, tree-shaded streets and period-style lamps, giving it a self-contained, almost secluded feel despite sitting minutes from the mall, Caltrain, and Highways 92 and 101.
