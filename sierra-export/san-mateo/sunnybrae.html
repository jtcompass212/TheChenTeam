Sunnybrae Homes & Real Estate

Just south of Parkside and centered on Norfolk Street, Sunnybrae — together with its 19th Avenue Park pocket — is one of San Mateo's original postwar suburbs. Local builders raised most of its 1940s–50s ranch homes, while Joseph Eichler added 232 California Modern homes nearby, prized today for their walls of glass, open floor plans, and central atria. It's a quieter, friendlier, all-American corner of the city that still delivers real value.
