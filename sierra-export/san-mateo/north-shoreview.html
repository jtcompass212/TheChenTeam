North Shoreview Homes & Real Estate

San Mateo's northernmost neighborhood, North Shoreview delivers genuine bay-waterfront living, anchored by Coyote Point Recreation Area's beach, trails, and marina, and by the CuriOdyssey science museum next door. Homes range from modest midcentury ranches to newer construction with real bay views, and the neighborhood's northern edge puts downtown San Mateo, Burlingame, and SFO all within easy reach.
