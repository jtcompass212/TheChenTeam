San Mateo Park Homes & Real Estate

Widely regarded as San Mateo's single most exclusive address, San Mateo Park is a grand, park-like enclave of early-1900s estate homes on winding, tree-canopied lanes — roughly bounded by the Hillsborough town line to the west, Barroilhet Avenue to the north, and El Camino Real to the east. Deep setbacks, mature landscaping, and architecture ranging from Tudor Revival to Mediterranean give it the feel of a private country club, all within a few minutes of downtown San Mateo.
