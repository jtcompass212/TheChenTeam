Millbrae · Millbrae Meadows

Millbrae Meadows Homes & Real Estate

Tucked behind Capuchino at the city's north-western corner, west of I-280, Millbrae Meadows is about as intact a piece of mid-century tract building as the Peninsula still has — consistent in scale, generous in setback, and largely unaltered at the street. Meadows Elementary and Meadows Park sit inside the neighborhood, which is a large part of why families who arrive here tend to stay.
