Millwood Homes & Real Estate

A handful of families grew flowers here for the San Francisco and Peninsula markets until Millbrae incorporated in 1948, after which the land was sold off in pieces and subdivided into the moderate one- and two-story houses that fill it today, most of them early 1950s. Green Hills Park, the Capuchino High campus and the country club form its edges — and in 1968 the neighborhood organized successfully against a freeway proposed straight through it.
