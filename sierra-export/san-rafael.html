About the Community

"The City With a Mission"

San Rafael takes its name and nickname from Mission San Rafael Arcángel, founded in 1817 as a hospital mission for the Coast Miwok. As the largest city and county seat in Marin, it's also the most ethnically diverse community in the county, with a genuinely vibrant restaurant scene along Fourth Street unmatched anywhere else north of the Golden Gate.

History

The 20th mission and the Angel of Healing

Once home to Coast Miwok villages, San Rafael takes its name from Mission San Rafael Arcángel, founded in 1817 as the 20th Spanish mission — used specifically as a hospital because of the area's ideal, sunny weather. The mission was abandoned after Mexico secularized California's missions in 1834, and eventually fell into ruin; the current mission building dates to 1949.

San Rafael incorporated on February 18, 1874, and became Marin County's seat. Wealthy San Franciscans built grand homes near the original mission site in the 1870s and 1880s, and the arrival of the railroad in 1879 connected the city to the national rail system by 1888.
