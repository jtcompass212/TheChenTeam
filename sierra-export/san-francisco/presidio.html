Similar Neighborhoods

Presidio Heights

Presidio Heights sits west of Pacific Heights along the Presidio's southern wall, with large homes on quiet streets and Sacramento Street as its retail spine.

Sea Cliff

Sea Cliff sits on the bluffs above China Beach at the city's northwest corner, a small enclave of large detached houses on unusually generous lots.

Marina District

The Marina occupies the flat, filled ground along the northern waterfront, with Marina-style flats and homes on a regular grid.
