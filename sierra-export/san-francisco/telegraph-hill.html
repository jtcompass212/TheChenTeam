Similar Neighborhoods

North Beach

North Beach is the city's Italian quarter, dense and highly walkable, built around Washington Square and the Columbus Avenue corridor.

Russian Hill

Russian Hill rises steeply above Polk Street, with view flats and a small number of detached houses on some of the sharpest grades in the city.

North Waterfront

The North Waterfront runs along the Embarcadero north of the Ferry Building, built largely from converted piers and warehouses.
