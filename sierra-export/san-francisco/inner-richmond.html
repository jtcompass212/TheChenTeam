Inner Richmond Homes & Real Estate

The Inner Richmond is the district's most walkable end, sitting between Golden Gate Park and the Presidio with Clement Street running through its centre. Edwardian flats and small apartment buildings dominate, with detached houses appearing on the quieter northern blocks. Similar Neighborhoods

Central Richmond

Central Richmond is the flat, family-scaled middle of the district — a regular grid of Edwardian and marina-style homes on 25-foot lots, most with garages below.

Lone Mountain

Lone Mountain climbs the hill around the University of San Francisco at the district's southeast corner.

Lake Street

Lake Street runs along the Presidio's southern wall, a quiet corridor of wide lots and substantial pre-war homes.
