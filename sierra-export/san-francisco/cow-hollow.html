Cow Hollow Homes & Real Estate

Cow Hollow sits on the slope between the Marina and Pacific Heights, anchored by Union Street's shops and restaurants. The stock is a mix of Victorians, Edwardians and flats, on ground that rises steadily south. Below you’ll find current homes for sale in Cow Hollow, updated throughout the day.

Similar Neighborhoods

Marina District

The Marina occupies the flat, filled ground along the northern waterfront, with Marina-style flats and homes on a regular grid.

Pacific Heights

Pacific Heights runs along the crest above the northern waterfront and is the most expensive residential ground in San Francisco.

Presidio Heights

Presidio Heights sits west of Pacific Heights along the Presidio's southern wall, with large homes on quiet streets and Sacramento Street as its retail spine.
