About the Community

From stagecoach stop to Alphabet's hometown

Mountain View incorporated in 1902, named for its views of the Santa Cruz Mountains. Today it's home to the Googleplex, NASA's Ames Research Center at Moffett Field, and Shoreline Park — a former landfill and pig farm transformed into 750 acres of parkland and an outdoor amphitheater in the 1990s.

History

From railroad depot to tech capital

Mountain View began as an agricultural stagecoach stop before the San Francisco and San Jose Railroad's 1864 arrival shifted the town center to a new depot near Castro Street, named for an early landowning family. The city incorporated on November 7, 1902.

Moffett Field, developed after 1931 for U.S. Navy airships, brought federal research investment that continues today through NASA Ames. Between 1950 and 1960 alone, the city's population grew nearly fourfold with the rise of the aerospace and electronics industries — the direct precursor to today's technology economy.
