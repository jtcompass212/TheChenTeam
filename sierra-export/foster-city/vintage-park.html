Vintage Park Homes & Real Estate

Vintage Park is Foster City’s office district rather than a residential neighborhood. The area around Vintage Park Drive and Chess Drive is commercial — the Gilead Sciences campus is the largest occupant — so there is no housing stock or sales history to report for it. If you are looking to live near this end of the city, the nearest residential neighborhoods are Treasure Isle and Bay Vista.
