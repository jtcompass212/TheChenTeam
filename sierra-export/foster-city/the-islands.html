Foster City · The Islands

The Islands Homes & Real Estate

One of the smallest neighborhoods in Foster City, The Islands is a handful of blocks between Shell Boulevard and the water, and it is defined by a single development: a 174-unit condominium complex that won a 1976 Award of Excellence from Architectural Record. It is a rare thing on the Peninsula — multifamily housing from the 1970s that people still single out for how it was designed rather than in spite of it.
