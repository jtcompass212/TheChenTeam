Foster City · Sea Colony

Sea Colony Homes & Real Estate

The largest neighborhood in Foster City and the one most completely built around the water, Sea Colony fills the southeastern corner where the lagoon system is at its widest. Sea Cloud Park sits inside it, home to the Foster City Little League, and the combination of open space and waterfront frontage keeps it among the most sought-after addresses in the city.
